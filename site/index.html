What awaits you in space

4 worlds, 2,000 levels

Asteroid Belt, Frozen Nebula, Lava Galaxy and Void Core. Every world has its own enemy ships, background and music, and they open in order.

20 secret worlds

Hidden between the levels. Find them to win diamonds and special rewards.

Ships and weapons

A huge shop with ships, auras, avatars and upgrades. The pricier the ship, the stronger and prettier its shots, all the way up to Supernova.

Clubs with ranks

Leader, co-leader, senior and member. Each club has a logo and a chat with word filters, and you can see who is online and who is in a game room.

Play with friends

Open a room, invite friends and play the same level together in real time. You can also ask to join a friend who is already playing.

Cloud save

All progress is saved to your account. Sign in on any device with your @name and an email code and continue where you left off.

Four worlds

500 levels in each world. Finish one and the next opens.

FAQ

Is the game really free?

Yes. No payments and no ads. You earn every coin and diamond by playing.

How do I switch devices without losing progress?

On the new device choose "I have an account" and sign in with your @name and email. A sign-in code arrives by email and all your progress loads from the cloud.

Can I play with someone who isn't on my friends list?

Yes. Being in the same club is enough, or just send them the room code.

Windows shows a warning. Is that OK?

Yes. The game is new and not yet known to SmartScreen. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
